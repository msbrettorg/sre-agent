Metric definitions

Observed reservations include every service with CommitmentDiscountType = Reservation, without a compute-only filter. Savings plans remain separate. Cost utilization = used / total amortized effective commitment cost. Waste = unused / total. These are cost-weighted measures, not hours or purchased-capacity utilization. Commitment purchase charges are excluded. Observed benefit IDs do not establish purchased or active inventory. Missing or invalid costs, unrecognized allocation statuses, and zero denominators make the affected percentage unavailable. Reported costs may be delayed or revised; reported days do not establish complete ingestion.

Observed cost utilization normalizes only negative floating-point residuals within 0.000000001 billing-currency units of zero. Larger negative costs remain invalid. Service spend share uses reservation-backed and savings-plan-backed EffectiveCost / total non-Spot usage EffectiveCost for each service, account, profile, and currency; purchases and unused commitment costs are excluded. This is observed spend attribution, not eligible-usage coverage. No global duplicate audit is asserted for this service aggregate.

Reservation native usage share = reservation-backed ConsumedQuantity / total ConsumedQuantity within the same SKU, meter, region, unit, pricing block, license type, and billing scope. Only meter populations with observed reservation usage are shown. Missing identities or units, invalid quantities, mismatched pricing quantities, and size-flex conversion requirements make that row unavailable. Percentages from unlike meter populations must not be combined.

VM usage coverage (vCPU-hours) = reservation-backed and savings-plan-backed consumed vCPU-hours / total consumed vCPU-hours for selected VM, VM Scale Sets, and Batch CPU meters. Reservations and savings plans are shown separately. This authored compute usage proxy is not all-eligible Compute coverage or commitment utilization. Spot, license, ancillary, purchase, and unused-commitment accounting rows are excluded. Invalid source quantities or benefit attribution make the affected result unavailable.

VM spend share (effective cost) = reservation-backed and savings-plan-backed EffectiveCost / total EffectiveCost for the same selected VM cohort and period. Both sides use the same amortized effective-cost basis, in each billing scope's currency. This is the share of observed spend attributed to benefits, not the share of hours, savings, or pre-discount eligible-usage coverage. Unused commitment cost is excluded. Missing, invalid, or negative costs, invalid benefit attribution, duplicate rows, or a zero denominator make spend share unavailable without replacing valid vCPU-hour coverage.

Savings-plan recommendation amounts are Azure estimates for the displayed evaluation period, billing profile, currency, and term. Reservation recommendation amounts are Advisor annual-savings estimates for the displayed subscription, product, term, and lookback; they do not supply evaluated purchase costs. Azure modeled coverage uses the savings-plan evaluated cost-without-benefit basis, not vCPU-hours or observed effective-cost spend share. One-year and three-year terms are alternatives. Modeled waste is included in total cost. Daily savings-plan utilization excludes the current incomplete UTC day.
